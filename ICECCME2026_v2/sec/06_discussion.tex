\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Main empirical takeaways}

The evaluated systems support two deployment rules. First, low proposal
length is preferred: increasing $k$ raises accepted block size but worsens
TTFT, TPOT, and total latency. Second, deterministic decoding is usually
faster than stochastic decoding at matched $k$. These rankings recur across
model families and devices, but their outcomes differ: RTX4090 achieves net
acceleration, whereas every RTX5090-laptop configuration is subbaseline.
Reported speedups should therefore be treated as properties of a complete
model--hardware--runtime stack, not of a GPU generation or policy alone.

\subsection{Novelty and contribution scope}

The contribution is empirical and systems-oriented, not a new decoding
algorithm. Frozen manifests, matched baselines, raw artifacts, and paired
statistics connect acceptance and token timing to practical operating choices
on two consumer GPU profiles.

\subsection{Variance and evaluation depth}

The corrected paired tests distinguish five significant Qwen3 RTX4090 gains
from the nonsignificant stochastic $k{=}16$ result. Repeat-seed evidence is
more limited: seeds 123 and 999 cover only $k{=}4$, although they preserve
regime ordering across both families and devices. CNN/DailyMail ROUGE-L,
BLEU, and BERTScore jointly reveal a quality cost that partially recovers as
$k$ increases, opposing the speed trend. These automated metrics do not fully
measure factuality or task utility.

\section{Threats to validity}
\label{sec:threats}

\textbf{Hardware and runtime dependence.}
The tested PyTorch/Transformers stack accelerates Qwen3 on RTX4090 but not on
RTX5090-laptop. Results cannot be extrapolated to other consumer or data-center
GPUs without target-stack measurement.

\textbf{Model-family scope.}
This study centers on same-family Qwen2.5 and Qwen3 target/draft pairings.
Cross-family speculative pairings may show different acceptance and quality
behavior.

\textbf{Evaluation breadth.}
The benchmark suite covers reasoning, knowledge QA, and summarization, but does
not exhaust long-context, safety, or factual consistency requirements.

\textbf{Sample-level versus seed-level dispersion.}
Mean$\pm$std values describe prompt dispersion within a seed. Repeat seeds
cover only $k{=}4$; full-grid multi-seed evidence remains future work.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Speculative decoding on consumer GPUs is strongly stack-dependent.
Deterministic $k{=}4$ is best on RTX4090, but the same policy family remains
slower than autoregressive decoding on RTX5090-laptop. Target-hardware
benchmarking is therefore required before deployment. Future work should
repeat the full grid across seeds, broaden model and hardware coverage, and
evaluate longer contexts and factuality-sensitive quality metrics.
